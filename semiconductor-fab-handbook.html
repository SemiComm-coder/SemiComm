The Semiconductor Fab Handbook

What does it actually take to build a semiconductor fab from the ground up?

Published: September 2, 2026 | Author: Ashish Rathore

This SemiComm white paper breaks down the technical, operational and economic requirements of a 300mm mature-node fab, using a representative 40,000 wafer-starts-per-month (WSPM) facility and a phased 45/40nm → 28nm technology strategy.

It connects the complete fab ecosystem, from technology selection, process flow and equipment to materials, utilities, workforce, yield, customers, supply chain and profitability. The analysis includes a planning CAPEX range of US$7–10 billion, roughly 2,500–3,000 employees, an 85% utilization target, and illustrative revenue potential of around US$1 billion per year.

What the handbook covers

  • Technology strategy: 180/130nm vs. 45/40nm vs. 28nm
  • Fab architecture and semiconductor process flow
  • Equipment and materials BOM
  • Power, water, gases and fab infrastructure
  • Workforce and operating model
  • Yield and cost-per-good-die economics
  • CAPEX, OPEX, utilization and profitability
  • Supply-chain and customer qualification risks
  • Greenfield, acquisition, JV and technology-transfer strategies

Core takeaway: A successful semiconductor fab is not simply a cleanroom filled with equipment. Sustainable manufacturing requires qualified customers, stable yield, high utilization, reliable infrastructure, disciplined operations and the right product mix working together.
